# PAYVITY — Amazon Paketshop Cinematic Customer Journey

## Production objective

Create a premium, photorealistic, human-led cinematic product demonstration for the PAYVITY Amazon Paketshop website.

Follow a customer from the moment she enters the shop until she leaves carrying her Amazon parcel.

This must feel like a real commercial filmed inside an operating Amazon Paketshop, presented through an Apple-inspired scroll-controlled website experience.

Do not create a motion graphics explainer, infographic, cartoon or floating smartphone presentation.

## Storyboard and timeline

**Scene 01 — 0–3 seconds: Arrival**

Show a wide cinematic shot of the shop entrance.

The customer opens the door, enters the shop and approaches the Amazon Hub counter.

The associate notices the customer and smiles.

Use newly generated or supplied live-action footage matching attachments 05 and 06.

**Scene 02 — 3–6 seconds: Greeting**

Show both women interacting naturally at the counter.

The customer greets the associate and takes out her green smartphone. She opens her Amazon pickup notification.

The associate takes out her black smartphone.

**Scene 03 — 6–9 seconds: Open the app**

Use an over-the-shoulder camera angle focusing on the associate's smartphone.

Insert attachment 01 as the actual smartphone display.

The associate taps the original menu option “Abholung durch den Kunden”.

Do not redraw the interface.

German overlay: “App öffnen.”

**Scene 04 — 9–12 seconds: Present the pickup code**

Use attachment 05 as the original photographic shot and reference for the human interaction.

The customer holds her smartphone towards the associate.

The associate positions her smartphone to scan the customer's barcode.

Maintain realistic hand movements and device positioning.

**Scene 05 — 12–15 seconds: Scan the pickup code**

Transition from the physical scanning interaction to attachment 02.

Use the exact original screenshot.

A brief, subtle scanning highlight may be added as an independent overlay without modifying the original image.

German overlay: “Abholcode scannen.”

**Scene 06 — 15–18 seconds: Retrieve the parcel**

Return to the real-world shop environment.

The associate locates the matching Amazon parcel in the storage area, picks it up and brings it to the counter.

The customer waits naturally.

Use the parcel and environment from attachment 06 as visual references.

**Scene 07 — 18–21 seconds: Scan the parcel**

Show the associate holding her smartphone above the parcel label.

Transition into attachment 03.

Preserve the original customer name, parcel information and barcode.

German overlay: “Paket finden und scannen.”

**Scene 08 — 21–23 seconds: Successful confirmation**

Reveal attachment 04 on the associate's smartphone.

The original “Done! Pickup completed” screen must remain unchanged.

Hold the shot briefly before returning to the human interaction.

**Scene 09 — 23–26 seconds: Parcel handover**

Use attachment 06 as the original counter shot and starting reference for the motion.

The associate lowers her smartphone, gently moves the Amazon parcel towards the customer and hands it over.

The customer receives the parcel with both hands.

Both women smile naturally.

German overlay: “Paket übergeben.”

**Scene 10 — 26–29 seconds: Customer departure**

Continue seamlessly from the handover.

The customer secures the parcel in her arms, thanks the associate, turns around and walks towards the shop exit.

Show her walking away naturally while carrying the same parcel.

The associate returns to her work.

Maintain full character, clothing and environmental continuity.

**Scene 11 — 29–30 seconds: Closing**

Show the customer leaving the shop with the parcel.

Hold the final cinematic composition and gently darken the background.

Closing headline:

“Wenige Schritte. Ein zufriedener Kunde.”

Supporting text:

“So einfach funktioniert die Paketabholung in Ihrem Geschäft.”

Reserve space for the website's native HTML CTA:

“Jetzt Paketshop werden”

## Cinematography

- Photorealistic live-action commercial aesthetic.
- Natural human anatomy and movements.
- Soft, realistic lighting.
- Smooth camera tracking and controlled push-ins.
- Natural close-ups of hands, smartphones and the parcel.
- Consistent color grading.
- Seamless transitions between physical interactions and original app screenshots.
- Minimalist German typography.
- Charcoal #080B10 and Amazon Hub blue #00A8E1 for external overlays only.

Avoid excessive effects, holographic screens, floating devices, cartoon characters, artificial facial expressions and unnecessary graphics.

## Website integration

The final experience must be controlled by scroll progress rather than independent autoplay.

Map the entire 30-second composition to scroll progress from 0 to 100%.

Scrolling down advances the story. Scrolling up reverses it.

Use a pinned viewport, smooth scroll scrubbing and responsive positioning.

The CTA must be an accessible and clickable native HTML element, not text embedded into the video.

Provide a reduced-motion fallback.

## Acceptance criteria

- All six original attachments are used.
- The Amazon Hub screenshots are preserved.
- The same two human characters appear throughout.
- The customer visibly enters the shop.
- The pickup code and parcel barcode are scanned in the correct order.
- Successful pickup confirmation appears before the handover.
- The customer physically receives the parcel.
- The customer turns, walks away and leaves with the parcel.
- No fictional app screens or features are introduced.
- The experience feels like a premium live-action commercial, not a slideshow.
