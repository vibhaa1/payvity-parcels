# PAYVITY cinematic journey

## Style Prompt
Restrained documentary commercial in the original shop. Real people, natural movements and original devices carry the narrative. Original app screenshots appear as legible editorial inserts. Crossfades last three native source frames. Photographs guide continuity and matching; they do not interrupt the live action.

## Colors
- #080B10: canvas and contained-media margins.
- #FFFFFF: website headlines and controls.
- #00A8E1: small website accent and progress indicator.
- #BDC6CF: secondary website copy.

## Typography
Segoe UI, with system sans-serif fallbacks, for native preview text. No typography is burned into the film. App typography belongs to the immutable original screenshots.

## What NOT to Do
- No floating smartphones, animated photographs or generated app screens.
- No motion interpolation, artificial slow motion or decorative transitions.
- No cropping away the actors' interaction to fill a portrait frame.
- No headlines or CTA baked into the exported film.
- No claim that the missing exit footage is complete.
